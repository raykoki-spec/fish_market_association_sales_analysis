PROJECT FINDINGS REPORT: KISUMU FISH MARKET ASSOCIATION

**Top vendors per revenue generation:** The first one is Achieng Fresh Fish (KES 218774.5). Second is Atieno Lakeside Traders (KES 213707.0). Third is Otieno & Sons (KES 190412.5). They account for 59% of total revenue generation. The top three vendors carries much of the market.

**Profitability per buyer type:** Retail earns higher average price per kg of KES 405, compared to KES 355.0 per kg  wholesale price. However, wholesale trading accounts for more revenue generation with most total kgs of fish sold (2041.6 kg) compared to 826.3 kgs of retail trade.

**Monthly Revenue Generation:** There is upward trend of revenue generation from month to month (KES 304042 -> 342929 -> 413053). This is due to growth in sales record each month (20 -> 32 -> 38 sales per month).

**Recommendation:** Encourage vendors to incorporate both wholesale and retail sales to strike the balance between selling more stock while also making more profit per stock.
